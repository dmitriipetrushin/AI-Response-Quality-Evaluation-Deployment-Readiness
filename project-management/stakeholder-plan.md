# Proposed stakeholder communication plan

All stakeholders below are fictional roles. This document proposes a communication schedule; no actual meetings, approvals, emails, or team participation are claimed. Dmitry Petrushin is the portfolio owner, and the repository is an independently built simulation.

| Audience | Proposed message / decision | Cadence | Channel and evidence | Accountable role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operations sponsor | Scope, cost assumptions, release recommendation, and exceptions | Kickoff, weekly, and final decision | One-page status report and decision record | AI Project Manager |
| People Operations | Policy scope, reference answers, unknown benefits, privacy boundaries | Week 1 workshop; policy-change review | Versioned handbook and case comments | Policy owner |
| Security lead | High-risk scenarios, credential handling, critical failures, escalation | Before testing; immediate critical finding | Restricted issue record and critical-case evidence | Security lead |
| Finance | Expense thresholds, purchase splitting, supplier payment controls, estimate | Weeks 1 and 3; critical financial finding immediately | Approval matrix and budget review | Finance approver |
| Engineer and QA | Dependencies, data schema, reproducible checks, defects | Twice weekly in the proposed schedule | Backlog, test output, and report fingerprints | AI Project Manager |
| Employee representatives | Clarity, expected assistant behavior, escalation, feedback | Proposed week 3 usability session | Synthetic scenario walkthrough and feedback log | Operations sponsor |
| Decision group | Gate results, remaining uncertainty, no-go or bounded-pilot approval | Week 4 | Executive report, evidence links, explicit signatures in a future project | Operations sponsor |

A future critical finding immediately suspends release consideration, alerts the relevant risk owner, and triggers a documented remediation plan. A material policy change requires policy-owner confirmation and regression review. No dashboard percentage substitutes for this process.

The executive report distinguishes completed engineering work, simulated labels, proposed business actions, and unverified integration steps. A future decision log should record date, evidence version, approver role, decision, conditions, and follow-up owner. No invented sign-off record is included in this repository.
